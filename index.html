Straight answers

The things people ask first.

Why is it only $150? What's the catch?

No catch. I work fast, I don't have an office, and I don't have a sales team to pay for. An agency charges thousands because they have thirty people between you and whoever actually builds the thing. Here that's just me.

What if I don't like it?

Then you don't pay. You see the finished site working before any money changes hands. If it's not right I'll fix it, and if it's still not right we shake hands and you owe nothing.

Do I own the site?

All of it. The domain is in your name, the hosting account is yours, and the files are yours. I'm not renting it to you and you're not locked in — if you want to move it somewhere else later, you can.

I don't have a domain name yet.

I'll help you pick one and get it set up. You pay the registrar directly — usually about $12 a year — so it stays in your name, not mine. Hosting for a site like this is free.

Can you change things later?

Yes. Small stuff — new hours, a phone number, swapping a photo — send me a message and it's done same day. Bigger additions like a whole new page we'll price when you need it.

How fast, really?

48 hours from the moment you send me your details. The clock starts when I have your photos, hours and text — not when you first message me. Send everything at once and it's usually faster.
