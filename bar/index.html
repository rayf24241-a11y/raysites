The room

Forty seats and a rule about phone calls.

We opened in 2018 in what used to be a print shop. The bar is the original counter, sanded back about nine times. There's no sign — the door is the one with the brass handle, between the florist and the laundry.

Take calls outside. Everything else is fine.
